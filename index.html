Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries

What are the leasing requirements?

+

Westfield Dominion welcomes retailers of all sizes. For luxury flagship spaces, we require established brands with proven market presence. Contemporary and lifestyle spaces have flexible requirements. Contact our leasing team for specific criteria.

How can I host an event at Westfield Dominion?

+

We offer versatile spaces for brand activations, conferences, concerts, and cultural events. Our 150,000 sqm of event space can be configured for events of any size. Contact our events team to discuss your vision and availability.

What sponsorship opportunities are available?

+

We offer multiple sponsorship tiers with access to 150M+ annual visitors. Packages include branding, event naming rights, exclusive partnerships, and premium positioning. Contact partnerships team for custom packages.

Is Westfield Dominion accessible to visitors with disabilities?

+

Yes, Westfield Dominion is fully accessible. We provide wheelchair access, accessible parking, accessible restrooms, and assistance services. For specific accessibility needs, please contact us at info@westfield-dominion.com.

What are the operating hours?

+

Westfield Dominion operates daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Extended hours are available during peak seasons and special events. Restaurant and entertainment venues may have different operating hours.
